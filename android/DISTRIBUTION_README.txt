======================================================
SHIA MARKAZ - ANDROID INSTALLATION & PLAY STORE GUIDE
======================================================

1. DIRECT INSTALLATION ON ANDROID DEVICES (Sideload):
   - File: apk/Shia-Markaz-DirectInstall.apk
   - Compatibility: Android 7.0 (API 24) to Android 15 (API 35)
   - Instructions:
     1. Download Shia-Markaz-DirectInstall.apk directly to your Android device.
     2. Open your device's Files or Downloads app.
     3. Tap on the APK file.
     4. If prompted, select "Settings" and toggle "Allow from this source".
     5. Tap "Install". The complete Shia Markaz app is now installed!

2. GOOGLE PLAY STORE CONSOLE UPLOAD:
   - File: bundle/Shia-Markaz-PlayStore.aab
   - Format: Android App Bundle (.aab)
   - Application ID: com.tafseerenamoona.app
   - Instructions:
     1. Open Google Play Console (https://play.google.com/console).
     2. Select your application or click "Create app".
     3. In the left navigation, go to "Release" -> "Production" (or "Testing" -> "Internal testing").
     4. Click "Create new release".
     5. Drag and drop Shia-Markaz-PlayStore.aab into the "App bundles" upload area.
     6. Complete your release notes, click "Next", and then "Save and publish".
